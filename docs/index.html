Bengals at Falcons in Madrid, Sun 8 Nov, 10:30 pm Perth, on NFL Net. Cincy is 5-2 and laying 3 at the Bernabeu, total 52.5. Atlanta is 3-5 and coming off a 16-0 shutout loss at Tampa Bay.

  • Burrow is chasing a fourth straight win. He went 38/48 for 462 yards, 5 TD and 3 INT in the 43-36 overtime win over Tennessee.
  • Michael Penix Jr. went 8/24 for 125 yards and 4 INT in the Falcons' last outing, a 16-0 loss at Tampa Bay.
  • ESPN's preview frames it as Burrow's three-game streak against Penix and the Falcons in Madrid.
  • Pittsburgh sits 6-2 and leads the division, so Cincinnati cannot bank on sloppy wins piling up.

Watch for

  1. Turnovers. Cincinnati gave it away four times against Tennessee, three of them Burrow picks, and still won.
  2. The run game. 19 carries for 75 yards at 3.9 a pop last week, with Chase Brown at 12 for 55.
  3. Red zone work. Bengals were 5-6 inside the 20 against the Titans, and that efficiency carried a leaky defense.

The call

Bengals cover the 3 and the game goes over 52.5. Cincinnati put up 510 yards last week and Atlanta just got shut out with a four-interception day from its quarterback.

Cincinnati won the yardage fight, 510 to 440, and kept converting: 7-of-10 on third down, 5-of-6 in the red zone. Four turnovers nearly gave it away before Burrow ran one in to settle it 43-36 in OT.

  • Joe Burrow: 38/48, 462 yards, 5 TD, 3 INT, plus 3 carries for 10 yards and the OT rushing score. Volatile, but he carried it.
  • Tee Higgins: 10 catches, 155 yards, 2 TD. Ja'Marr Chase added 11 for 141 and a score after, per ESPN, spiking his helmet in frustration early.
  • Tennessee's Tony Pollard went for 21 carries, 117 yards, 2 TD against a Bengals front that gave up 5.6 a carry.

Pittsburgh stays ahead at 6-2 after hammering Cleveland 27-6. Cincinnati sits 5-2 on a three-game run with the best point differential in the North at +48. Cleveland 5-3, Baltimore 4-4 and sliding.

The take

A 1-7 team hung 36 on you and the defence allowed 6.3 a play. Ben Baby's line about needing a complete game is right. Burrow's three picks against Atlanta's level of team is survivable, elsewhere it is not.
