Bengals at Baltimore, Mon 1:00 am Perth, M&T Bank Stadium, sunny and 16C. Cincinnati is 3-2 and laid as a 1.5 point road favourite with a 48.5 total. Baltimore is 4-2 but off a 10-13 loss in Cleveland.

  • Lamar Jackson says he has a high ankle sprain and is unsure if he plays, per ESPN. He has missed the past two games but is "heading in the right direction".
  • Trey Hendrickson faces his old team for the first time since signing with Baltimore. ESPN's preview says he is finally looking healthier.
  • Ben Baby reports both Hendrickson and the Bengals are downplaying the matchup.
  • Burrow went 23/32 for 190 and 4 TD in the 31-24 win at Miami. Efficient, not explosive, at 5.9 yards per pass.

Watch for

  1. Cincinnati's run defence. Miami ran for 217 on 34 carries at 6.4 a pop and still lost.
  2. Third down. The Bengals were 9-13 in Miami and will need that rate again if Baltimore controls the clock.
  3. The Hendrickson snap count and who Cincinnati slides help to on his side.

The call

Bengals cover the 1.5. If Jackson is limited or out, Baltimore's offence stalls like it did in the 10-point day at Cleveland, and Burrow only needs an average game to win it.
