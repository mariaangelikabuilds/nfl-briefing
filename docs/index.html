Bengals at Washington, Tuesday 9:15am Perth on ESPN, CIN -6 with the total at 54.5. Cincinnati is 7-2 and riding a five-game win streak into a 3-5-1 home side. Intermittent clouds, 52F.

  • Burrow was a full participant at practice for the second straight day despite a bug, and per ESPN he is optimistic he will be at full strength for Monday night.
  • ESPN headline has Stephen A. calling Burrow the MVP front-runner. The 35/44, 409 yard, 4 TD night against Pittsburgh is the evidence.
  • ESPN's Ben Baby frames Amarius Mims and the offensive line as an underrated reason Burrow is on that trajectory.
  • John Keim previewed how Washington's blitz holds up against Burrow. That is the whole game in one question.

Watch for

  1. How Washington answers Ja'Marr Chase after an 11 rec, 231 yard night. Double him and Tee Higgins plus Chase Brown out of the backfield get the space.
  2. Cincinnati's penalties. Eight for 80 yards against Pittsburgh is the kind of thing that keeps a 3-5-1 team alive.
  3. The run game. 78 yards on 20 carries last week, 3.9 a pop. If that stays flat, it is all on Burrow again.

The call

Bengals cover the 6 and the game goes over 54.5. Burrow has thrown for 409 with 4 touchdowns and no picks against a better defense, and Cincinnati went 5-6 in the red zone doing it.
