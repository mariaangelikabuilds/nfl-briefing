Two 2-1 teams at Paycor, Mon 5 Oct 1:00 am Perth, CBS. Cincinnati is a 2.5-point home favourite with a 51.5 total. Mostly sunny, 21C, so no weather excuse for either passing game.

  • Bengals are coming off a 30-27 loss at Pittsburgh where Burrow went 28/37 for 282 and 3 TD with no picks, and the offence still lost two fumbles.
  • ESPN's preview headline frames it as Burrow staying unbeaten against Trevor Lawrence whenever their teams have met.
  • Jacksonville arrives off a 35-6 home win over New England. An ESPN headline asks whether Brian Thomas Jr., targeted once in Week 3, bounces back here.
  • Cleveland is 3-1 on a W3 run after beating Pittsburgh 27-24, so Cincinnati at 2-1 is already chasing in the North.

Watch for

  1. Ball security. Two fumbles lost in Pittsburgh was the difference in a three-point game.
  2. The run game. Cincinnati managed 83 yards on 18 carries while Pittsburgh ran for 138 on 23.
  3. Third down. The Bengals were 6-12 last week and 2-2 on fourth down, which is a lot of living on the edge.

The call

Bengals win but not by the number. Burrow is 3 TD and no picks sharp, yet the run game and the fumbles say this is a one-score game. Take Jacksonville +2.5.
