Bengals 2-0 into Acrisure at 1:00 am Perth Monday, laying CIN -3.5 with a 42.5 total. Pittsburgh is 1-1 off a 20-3 loss at New England. Low-scoring shape, and Cincinnati's defense just held Houston to 6.

  • Cincinnati's Week 2 win was defense first: Houston went 0-4 in the red zone and 0-4 on fourth down while piling up 374 yards.
  • ESPN reports Andrei Iosivas is expected to miss four to six weeks with a thumb injury, thinning the receiver room behind Chase and Higgins.
  • Per ESPN, Chase and Higgins are unhappy with NFL fines over a celebration and dispute the league's reading of it.
  • Headline from the game preview: Aaron Rodgers says he isn't worried about the Steelers' resolve after an uneven start. Joey Porter Jr.'s return date is still TBD.

Watch for

  1. Burrow's protection. He was sacked five times for 25 yards in Houston and the offense managed 4.5 yards per play.
  2. Third down. Cincinnati went 5-15 in Week 2 and took 12 penalties for 78 yards. That does not travel well to Pittsburgh.
  3. Chase Brown's workload. Twenty carries for 80 yards last week, 3.7 a pop, with the passing game short a body.

The call

Bengals win but do not cover the 3.5. Take the under 42.5 too. Two defenses, a Steelers team that just scored 3, and a Cincinnati offense averaging 267 yards is not a shootout.
