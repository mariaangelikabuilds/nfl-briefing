Two 6-2 teams, both on W4, meet at Paycor for the AFC North lead. Cincinnati is home and laying 3.5 with a total of 49.5. Mostly cloudy, 43F, so conditions should not rewrite either plan.

  • Pittsburgh and Cincinnati are tied at 6-2, though the Bengals hold the better point differential at +65 to +36.
  • ESPN's primer frames it as a battle for the division, Aaron Rodgers against Joe Burrow on Sunday night.
  • Ben Baby reported the Bengals are confident heading into the matchup, per the headline.
  • Cleveland sits 5-4 on an L2 and Baltimore is 4-5 on an L4, so the winner here can start pulling clear.

Watch for

  1. Ja'Marr Chase after his 10 rec, 155 yds, 1 TD in Madrid. How much attention Pittsburgh gives him shapes everything else.
  2. Cincinnati's third down work. They went 6-10 against Atlanta and had zero turnovers.
  3. The run game split. Chase Brown had 9 car, 57 yds and Samaje Perine 11 car, 43 yds last week.

The call

Bengals cover the 3.5. Burrow was 24/29 for 271 and 3 TD last out, the offense is at 7.1 yards per play form, and Pittsburgh has not faced that version of Cincinnati this year.
